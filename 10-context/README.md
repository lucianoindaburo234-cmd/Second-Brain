# 10-context

**Stable facts about me and my world.**

Background an assistant (or a future me) needs in order to give useful answers
without re-asking: who I am, what I'm working on, my tools and stack,
preferences, constraints, goals, key people, recurring commitments.

Belongs here:
- Personal and professional profile, current goals and priorities
- Environment: hardware, software, accounts, conventions I follow
- Standing preferences ("always do X", "never do Y")
- People and organizations I interact with regularly

Doesn't belong here:
- Reference knowledge about the world → `20-wiki`
- Work with a deliverable and an end date → `30-projects`

Keep entries short, current, and factual. Update in place rather than appending
history — stale context is worse than none.
